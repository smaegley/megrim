### New
- **"No migraine today" check-ins.** One tap on the Log tab, an empty day on the History
  calendar, or a button on the daily reminder records a clear day, with optional sleep, stress,
  triggers and foods. Clear days show a tick on the calendar.
- **Days tracked** and **Migraine days vs clear days** on Analytics. Once you've tracked 30 days
  (with at least 5 migraine days and 5 clear ones), Megrim compares your sleep, stress, triggers
  and foods on the days migraines started against clear days, as odds ratios. How it works:
  [docs/METHODS.md](https://github.com/smaegley/megrim/blob/main/docs/METHODS.md).
- **Daily reminder** (optional): a notification at a time you choose, with *No migraine today*
  and *Log* buttons. It skips a day you've already logged.
- **Migraine in progress** notification (Android, optional): a running clock and an *End*
  button while a migraine is open.
- **Symptoms**, **impact** (how much it affected your usual activities) and **attacks without headache**,
  such as aura-only or vestibular attacks, with their own Analytics cards.
- **Medications:** classes for acute medications, medication days per month by class, and a calm
  notice when the last three complete months each reached the ICHD-3 level for
  medication-overuse headache. **Preventive medications** with start and stop dates, marked on
  the monthly chart. **Relief tried** (dark room, cold pack…) and a **What helped** card.
- All of it is in the **PDF report**, the JSON export and import
  ([format](https://github.com/smaegley/megrim/blob/main/docs/IMPORT.md)).

### Also

  future day).
- Refreshed store screenshots.

### Upgrading
The database upgrades in place (schema v3 → v6); nothing you've logged changes.

### Permissions (Android)
`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (asked for only when you turn on the reminder or the Migraine in progress
notification), `VIBRATE`, and `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` (so the reminder survives a restart).
Neither notification is shown on a secure lock screen. There is still no location permission,
no account, and no network use unless you turn on weather.

Megrim is a personal diary, not a medical device.
