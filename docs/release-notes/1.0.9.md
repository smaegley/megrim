### New
- **Dates and times follow your phone's region.** In the US an entry reads "Mon, Nov 2, 2026,
  2:15 PM"; in the UK "Mon, 2 Nov 2026, 14:15". Times follow your phone's 12- or 24-hour
  setting, and the History calendar starts the week on Monday where that's the custom. Numbers
  use your region's separators. The PDF report follows the same formats.
- **Ready for translation.** Every piece of text in the app, including the PDF report, now
  comes from a single message file. Volunteers can translate Megrim in the browser on
  [Hosted Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/megrim/) (guide:
  [docs/TRANSLATING.md](https://github.com/smaegley/megrim/blob/main/docs/TRANSLATING.md)).
  The app is still in English: each language is added in a later release once it's complete
  and reviewed. Your exports don't change with the language.

### Fixed
- Sleep hours accept a decimal comma ("7,5"), as typed on many keyboards outside the US. Before,
  the value was dropped.

### Also
- iOS: the Donate link is no longer shown in the iPhone app, as the App Store allows such
  links only in the US. Android and F-Droid keep it.
- Refreshed store screenshots.
