Weather enrichment is now opt-in.

• Weather, pressure and air-quality lookups only happen if you turn them on (during setup or in Settings). Left off, Megrim makes no automatic network requests at all.
• IMPORTANT for existing users: this update leaves weather enrichment OFF until you enable it in Settings › Weather enrichment. Turning it on backfills weather for entries you already logged.
• Charts that need weather now say so instead of just looking empty.
• You can set your home location without any online search: type GPS coordinates or a Plus Code and Megrim decodes it on-device.
• Removed unused location permissions — the app declares only INTERNET and never reads your device location.
